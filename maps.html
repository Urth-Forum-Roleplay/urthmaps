Below are all the maps maintained by the Cartography Department. You can click the map names for additional information. The images on this page are for demonstration purposes only and are not kept up-to-date.

We recommend GIMP to edit XCF files and Inkscape to edit SVG files.

Please note that your browser may warn you when downloading files. This is due to the low volume of downloads and should not be cause for concern.

Political

(View - Source)

The primary map of Urth, featuring nations, cities, and subdivisions. All other maps are derived from this one.

Global Locator

(View - Source)

This map is used to highlight a nation or area on wiki pages (also known as a "locator map") from a global perspective. The colour used for the highlighted region is #346733.

Local Locator

(View - Source)

This map is used to highlight smaller area or a city on wiki pages (also known as a "locator map"). Unlike the previous map, this one is used at a more local level (closer to the subject). The colour used for the highlighted region is #c22335.

Topography

The topographical map of Urth, featuring mountains, valleys, and plains. The source file for this map is currently unavailable.

Climate

(View - Source)

The climatological map of Urth. It uses the Köppen climate classification. We are in the process of reworking all of our climate maps and hope to provide them to you in the near future.
