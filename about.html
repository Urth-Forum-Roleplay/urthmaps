urthmaps.com acts as a hub for downloads and information pertaining Urth Cartography.

What is Urth?

Urth is a present-day geopolitical roleplaying community that has existed for over two decades. It spawned from The East Pacific (TEP) community, which is based around the government simulation game NationStates.

Urth is the name of the planet the roleplay takes play on. The community maintains a world wiki, writes roleplays on the TEP forums, and communicates on Discord.

Can I join?

Absolutely! You can consult our guide for new players, which will have all the information you'll need to get started.

What is the Urth Cartography Department?

People have been maintaining the map since the very beginning of the roleplay community, but the Cartography Department was officially established in 2016 for that purpose. It is a composed of a team of volunteers who update the maps of Urth, write documentation, vote on new claims, help players create their own maps, etc. If it has something to do with a map, the Cartography Department is probably in charge of it.
